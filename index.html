What is cluster housing perumnas?
Cluster housing perumnas is a housing concept that consists of several houses in a gated cluster. Cluster housing perumnas offers comfortable, secure, and affordable housing with various complete and modern facilities.
Where is the location of cluster housing perumnas?
Cluster Housing Perumnas is strategically located in a well-developed residential area with easy access to main roads, public transportation, schools, shopping centers, and other essential facilities. The location is designed to provide a comfortable living environment while remaining well-connected to the city.
How much are the prices and types of houses in cluster housing perumnas?
Cluster Housing Perumnas offers a variety of house types to suit different needs and budgets. Prices vary depending on the house size, type, and selected features. For detailed pricing information and available unit types, please contact our sales team or visit our official marketing office.
What are the facilities provided in cluster housing perumnas?
Cluster Housing Perumnas is equipped with modern facilities to support a safe and comfortable lifestyle.
